# Music player

A personal desktop music player for my own YouTube playlists, with a Winamp-style look.

It plays music through YouTube's official embedded player and reads playlists through the YouTube Data API. Everything runs on my own computer: a Next.js app with a Postgres database, wrapped in an Electron window.

## Features

- My YouTube playlists in a library: pinned playlists, my own order, music and video sections
- A queue: play next, add to queue, drag to reorder
- Radio from any song, which keeps going with similar songs
- Discover: new songs based on what I listen to
- Search with Ctrl+F across playlists, albums and songs
- Save to playlist, new playlists, rename, move and remove songs
- Go to album, play history, song lengths and covers
- Mini player and picture-in-picture

## Key and BPM data

Song key and tempo (BPM) data comes from [GetSongBPM](https://getsongbpm.com).
